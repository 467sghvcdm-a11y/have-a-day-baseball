06

Two-out questions Quick answers

FAQ · 6 ITEMS
Q.01When exactly does Have a Day open?
Fall 2026 in El Cajon, East County San Diego. Founding members will get the exact opening date and an invite to the soft-open weekend before public hours start.
Q.02Is the $1 today the only charge?
Yes. We authorize a $1 hold to verify the card on file. The first charge based on your selected tier happens on opening day. Cancel anytime before and the hold is released.
Q.03What's actually included in the membership?
Two tiers — 30 gives you one 30-min lane reservation per day, 60 gives you one hour reservation per day. Additional lane time is available at the standard rate. Coaching is priced separately at $99/hr, same for members and non-members.
Q.04What happens after the 12 months are up?
Your rate moves to the standard rate at month 13 — $199/mo for 30, $299/mo for 60. Cancel anytime, no fee. No auto-upgrades, no surprise charges.
Q.05Why no coaches at launch?
We're building the coaching roster now. We'd rather open with the right coaches than fill slots with the wrong ones. Hitting, catching, and pitching instruction will be available once we've found people we trust with your athlete. When they're ready, you'll hear about it first as a founding member.
Q.06Can I really email from the lounge?
Yes. Fast Wi-Fi, real outlets, work-height tables, and clear views into every cage from one seat. We figure if we're asking you to be here three nights a week, you should be able to keep your day going.
